Detailed Description

Shared mDNS (RFC 6762) wire-format helpers for TinymDNS.

Header-only encoder/decoder for the small subset of DNS needed by multicast DNS: A-record queries and responses, label encoding and name decompression. Used by TinymDNSClient and TinymDNSServer.

Author
Phil Schatzmann
